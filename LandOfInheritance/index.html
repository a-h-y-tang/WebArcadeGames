The land is divided!

Joshua cast lots at Shiloh and marked out an inheritance for each tribe of Israel. Every boundary stone on the map carries a number: the count of squares in that tribe’s field.

  • Mark out one rectangular field around each stone.
  • A field must hold exactly one stone, and as many squares as the number on it.
  • Fields may never overlap, and when the land is divided no square is left over.

Mouse: drag from corner to corner to mark a field; click or right-click inside a field to clear it. Keyboard: arrow keys move, Space sets a corner and Space again marks the field, Esc cancels, Backspace clears. H hint, N new land, R reset.
