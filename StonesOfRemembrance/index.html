Memorial set up!

When Israel came up out of the Jordan, twelve men each carried a stone from the riverbed and set it up at Gilgal, so that later generations would ask what the stones meant. Walk the bearer across the dry bed and push every stone onto a memorial place.

  • You can only push a stone, never pull it — so you must stand on the far side of a stone to move it.
  • One stone at a time: a stone will not shift if a wall or another stone is behind it.
  • A stone shoved into a corner is wedged for good. Take it back with Undo.
