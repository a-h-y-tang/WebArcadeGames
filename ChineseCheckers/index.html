Chinese Checkers

Race your ten pegs across the star — one hop at a time, or a chain of jumps all the way over.

Your move — pick a green peg.

You win!

Both sides hold ten pegs in opposite points of the star. You play the green pegs at the bottom and win by filling the top point; the computer comes the other way. Nothing is ever captured.

  • Step one hole into any of the six directions, if it is empty.
  • Jump straight over a peg of either colour into the empty hole beyond — and keep jumping as long as you have a jump left, never landing twice on the same hole.
  • A whole chain of jumps is one move. Stop early whenever you like.

Mouse: click a peg, then click a ringed hole. Keyboard: arrow keys move the cursor, Space picks a peg or moves it, Esc deselects or ends a chain of jumps, U undo, N new game.
