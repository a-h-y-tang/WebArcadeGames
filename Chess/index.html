How to play

Click a piece to pick it up — its legal moves appear as dots, and a ring marks a piece you can take. Click one of them to move there. Click the piece again, or press Esc, to put it down.

  • A pawn that reaches the far rank opens a chooser: queen, rook, bishop or knight.
  • To castle, move the king two squares toward the rook; the rook follows by itself.
  • Undo (or U) takes back your move and the computer's reply. Flip Board turns it around, which helps when two people share a screen.
  • A king in check glows red. The game ends on checkmate, stalemate, the fifty-move rule, threefold repetition, or when neither side has enough material to mate.

Strength: Gentle looks one move ahead and varies its choice, Steady looks three, and Sharp looks four and takes a moment over it. Set Opponent to Two players to play both sides at one keyboard.
