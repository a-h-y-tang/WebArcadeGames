Bezalel was the craftsman the Lord filled with his Spirit to cut stone and carve timber for the tabernacle. Seven tiles lie on his bench: two large triangles, a medium triangle, two small triangles, a square and a parallelogram.

  • Fill the shadowed figure with all seven tiles.
  • No gaps and no overlaps — the tiles cover the figure exactly.
  • Every tile is used in every figure.

Mouse: drag a tile to lay it; it snaps to the grid. Drop a tile below the figure to put it back on the bench. Click a tile to select it. Keyboard: 1–7 pick a tile, arrow keys move it, R turns it (Shift+R turns it back), F flips it, Backspace returns it to the bench. H hint, N next figure, Esc reset.
