The Narrow Path

Press Space or click Start

Tilt the board and gather every waymark.

A pilgrim’s stone rolls on a tilting wooden board. Tilt the board with the arrow keys or WASD — or hold the pointer on the board and it tilts that way — and let gravity do the walking.

  • Gather every waymark on the stage (“Set up waymarks” — Jeremiah 31:21).
  • Keep out of the ditches beside the road. Fall in and you lose one of your three journeys, and the stone goes back to the start of the stage.
  • With every waymark gathered the narrow gate swings open. Roll into it to finish the stage.
  • Four stages lie ahead, each narrower than the last. The quicker a stage, the bigger the bonus.

Space starts or carries on, P pauses, R starts the run again. There is always a way round a ditch.
