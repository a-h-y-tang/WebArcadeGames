On the third day God gathered the waters into one place and the dry land appeared. Every number on this map stands on a piece of dry land, and tells you how many squares that land covers. Shade in the sea around them.

  • Each piece of land holds exactly one number and is exactly that many squares.
  • Two pieces of land may never touch side by side — only corner to corner.
  • The sea must be gathered into one piece, every square of it joined to the rest.
  • The sea may never fill a whole square of four; still water of two by two is not allowed anywhere on the map.

Every map can be worked out by those four rules alone — there is never any need to guess, and Hint will name the rule that settles the next square.
