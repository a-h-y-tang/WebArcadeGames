Every answer is out of the Bible — people, places, books and things, from the ark to the empty tomb. Read a clue, write the answer into the numbered cells, and the letters shared between words will help you along.

Mouse: click a cell to write there, click it again to turn across ↔ down, or click a clue to jump to its first cell. Keyboard: letters write, Backspace rubs out, arrow keys move, Space turns the direction, Tab moves to the next clue, Esc puts the pen down. Because every letter key writes, Check, Reveal Letter, Reset and New Scroll are buttons.
