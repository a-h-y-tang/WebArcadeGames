Sparrow's Garden

Peck every seed in the garden — mind the cats.

A sparrow hops the gravel paths of a walled kitchen garden, gathering the seeds scattered along them. Four garden cats prowl the same paths.

  • Peck every seed (10 each) to clear the garden. A fresh, faster garden is laid out each time.
  • The four sunflower heads (50 each) turn the cats shy for a few seconds — reach a shy cat and it scampers home to the shed for 200, then 400, 800, 1600.
  • A berry (100) ripens in the middle of the garden once sixty seeds have gone.
  • A prowling cat that catches the sparrow startles it home. Three lives, and the cats get quicker every garden.

Arrow keys or W A S D to hop, P to pause. Turn early — the sparrow takes the turn at the next corner that allows it.
