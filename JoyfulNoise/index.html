Play a hymn of praise

Four instruments, four keys: D F J K. Strike every note as it reaches the line.

The notes of the hymn fall down four lanes — harp, lyre, timbrel and trumpet, low notes on the left and high notes on the right. Strike a lane as its note crosses the line and the instrument sounds that note of the melody, so a clean run plays the tune under your fingers.

  • On the line is Perfect (100), a little out is Great (60) and only just caught is Good (30).
  • Every note struck lengthens your combo; a note let slip ends it.
  • The hymn always plays to its last note — the end screen grades how much you caught.

Keys: D F J K or the arrow keys play the four lanes, and you can click or tap a lane instead. Space plays, R starts the hymn again, P pauses, and 1–3 choose the hymn.
