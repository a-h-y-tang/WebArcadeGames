Add a bone to either open end of the train so the touching numbers match. Empty your hand first and you win the hand, scoring the pips left in the computer's hand.

  • The highest double leads each hand — that bone is forced, so it is played for you.
  • Nothing playable? Draw a bone from the boneyard. Once the boneyard is empty you must Pass.
  • Two passes in a row block the train: the lighter hand wins and scores the heavier one.
  • First to 100 points takes the match.

Click a bone to play it. If it fits both ends, click one of the glowing pads beside the train. Keyboard: ← → to choose a bone, Enter to play it, L/R for a particular end, D to draw, P to pass.
