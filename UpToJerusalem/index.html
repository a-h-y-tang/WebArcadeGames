Joseph and Mary “went to Jerusalem every year at the Feast of the Passover” (Luke 2:41). Four families set out from four towns; you travel with Nazareth. Gather all four of your pilgrims in the temple courts before the other families do.

  • A pilgrim leaves home only on a six, stepping onto your start cell.
  • The die says how far one pilgrim walks. You choose which pilgrim goes.
  • Land on a lone pilgrim of another family and it goes back home — unless it stands on a well or a start cell, or has a companion beside it.
  • Roll a six, or send someone home, and you roll again. Three sixes in a row and your turn is over.
  • The last five cells are your own ascent, and the temple must be reached by an exact roll.

Mouse: click Roll, then click the pilgrim you want to send on. Keyboard: Space or Enter rolls, 1–4 picks a pilgrim, Enter takes the first move offered, N starts a new game.
