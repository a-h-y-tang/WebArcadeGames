Camp complete!

Israel kept the feast by living in shelters of palm branches. Pitch one booth beside each palm tree in the camp.

  • Every palm gets exactly one booth of its own, directly above, below, left or right of it — never diagonally.
  • No two booths may touch, not even at a corner.
  • The numbers down the left and across the top count the booths in that row or column.

Click an open square to pitch a booth, again to brush it to sand (ruled out), again to clear it. Right-click goes the other way.
