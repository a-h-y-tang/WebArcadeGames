King Hezekiah’s workmen cut a channel through solid rock to bring the water of the Gihon spring inside the city walls, into the Pool of Siloam. The spring is already running — lay the channel before the water catches up.

  • Sections arrive one at a time. Lay the next one on any bare ground; rock cannot be dug and a laid section cannot be moved.
  • When the countdown ends the water runs from the spring at a steady pace. Every cell it fills scores 10.
  • If the water meets bare ground, rock, or a section that does not face it, it breaks out and a day of digging is lost. Three lost days end the dig.
  • Water reaching the pool finishes the stretch for a 200 bonus. Five stretches complete the tunnel.

Mouse: click a cell to lay the next section. Keyboard: Arrow keys move the cursor, Space lays a section (and starts the dig), P pauses, N starts a new game.
