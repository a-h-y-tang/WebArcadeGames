A vineyard cannot be planted until the field is cleared. Keep the threshing stone in the air with your basket and knock every field stone loose — five fields, and the vines go in.

  • Loose stones come away in one hit, packed stones in two, boulders in three. Bedrock never shifts, so play the angles around it.
  • Where the stone lands on the basket decides where it goes next: the edges send it out wide, the middle sends it straight back up.
  • Some stones leave a gift behind — a wider basket, a steadier hand, three stones at once, sticky pitch to hold the stone, or an extra worker. Catch it with the basket.
