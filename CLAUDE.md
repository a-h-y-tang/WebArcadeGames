# WebArcadeGames

## Content policy (required for every new game)

This collection is published at wholesomefun.org and must stay family friendly.
Before you build or propose a game, check the idea against this list. Do not add,
propose, or open pull requests for games that include any of the following:

- **Gambling**: casino games (blackjack, poker, roulette, slots, craps, baccarat),
  betting, wagering, or chip or money stakes.
- **Alcohol**: bars, taverns, pubs, saloons, beer or other drinks served in a bar
  setting, bartenders, or drunkenness. This includes "Tapper"-style bar-serving
  games under any name or theme (Soda Tapper, Soda Rush, Soda Slinger, Soda Shack,
  Tap Rush and the like). These have been rejected; do not suggest them again.
- **Witchcraft or the occult**: witches, wizards casting spells, magic rituals,
  demons, devils, curses, séances, and similar themes.
- **Anything sleazy or suggestive**: sexual content, revealing outfits, crude
  humour, smoking or drug use.

If an idea is borderline, choose a different one. Also check the open and recently
closed pull requests so you don't propose a game that was already rejected.
