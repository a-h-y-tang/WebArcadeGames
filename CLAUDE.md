# WebArcadeGames

## Content policy

This collection is published at wholesomefun.org and must stay family friendly.
Do not add, propose, or open pull requests for games that include:

- **Gambling**: casino games (blackjack, poker, roulette, slots), betting, wagering, or chip/money stakes.
- **Alcohol**: bars, taverns, beer or other drinks served in a bar setting. This includes
  "Tapper"-style bar-serving games under any name (Soda Tapper, Soda Rush, Tap Rush, and so on).
- **Witchcraft or the occult**: witches, spells, demons, curses, and similar themes.
- **Anything sleazy or suggestive.**

If a game idea is borderline, pick a different idea.
